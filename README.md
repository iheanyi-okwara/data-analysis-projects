# 🚀 Featured Projects

## 📡 Telecom Network Outage & Reliability Analytics

![Telecom Executive Dashboard](Telecom_Network_Outage_Analytics/screenshots/01_Executive_Overview.png)

### Overview

End-to-end analysis of telecom network reliability across **500 network sites, 5 vendors, and 12 UK regions**, using outage incidents, alarms, SLA assessments, trouble tickets, customer complaints, and maintenance activity.

The project analyses **5,000 network outages** and **15,000 alarm records** to identify the operational drivers of network disruption and service performance.

### Key Findings

- **Hardware Failure** was the leading reliability issue, generating **1,076 outages** and **826.5 hours of downtime**.
- London accounted for approximately **26% of outages** and **27% of total downtime**.
- Overall formal SLA compliance was **80.50%**.
- Only **30.28% of alarms** were automatically resolved.
- Critical alarms recorded the longest average first-alarm time at **11.2 minutes**.
- Emergency maintenance recorded the lowest maintenance completion rate at **91.8%**.

### Tools

**SQL | Power BI | DAX | Data Modelling | PowerPoint | Git/GitHub**

## 🏥 NorthBridge Health Services Analytics

![NorthBridge Dashboard](NorthBridge_Analytics/screenshots/executive-dashboard.png)

### Overview

Healthcare administrative service-desk analytics project analysing ticket performance, SLA compliance, backlog, response times, resolution performance, escalation, and operational workload.

The project analyses **3,500 service tickets** across **114 agents and 72 clients** to identify service-delivery bottlenecks and opportunities for operational improvement.

### Key Findings

- **3,500 total service tickets** were analysed.
- **1,317 tickets** were resolved.
- Overall resolution rate was **37.6%**.
- **754 tickets (21.5%)** breached SLA.
- Average first-response time was **5.06 hours**.
- Average resolution time was **40.86 hours**.
- **2,180 open tickets** were already past SLA.
- Critical backlog reached **2,158 tickets**.

**SQL | Power BI | DAX | Excel | Data Modelling | Business Analysis**
