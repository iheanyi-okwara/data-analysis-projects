## 📡 Telecom Network Outage & Reliability Analytics

![Telecom Executive Dashboard](Telecom_Network_Outage_Analytics/screenshots/01_Executive_Overview.png)

### Overview

End-to-end telecom network reliability analysis covering **500 network sites, 5 vendors, and 12 UK regions**.

The project analyses **5,000 network outage incidents**, **15,000 alarm records**, SLA assessments, customer complaints, trouble tickets, and maintenance activity to identify the operational factors affecting network reliability and service performance.

### Key Findings

- **Hardware Failure** was the leading reliability issue, generating **1,076 outages** and **826.5 hours of downtime**.
- London accounted for approximately **26% of network outages** and **27% of total downtime**.
- Overall formal SLA compliance was **80.50%**.
- **4,750 of 5,000 outage incidents** had associated alarms.
- Only **30.28% of alarms** were automatically resolved.
- Critical alarms recorded the longest average first-alarm time at **11.2 minutes**.
- Customer complaint resolution rate was **84.55%**.
- Emergency maintenance recorded the lowest maintenance completion rate at **91.8%**.

### Business Value

The analysis identifies opportunities to improve **hardware reliability, regional network performance, vendor management, SLA compliance, alarm automation, high-priority ticket resolution, and preventive maintenance**.

**SQL | T-SQL | Power BI | DAX | Data Modelling | PowerPoint | Git/GitHub**

## 🏥 Horizon Health Network — Healthcare Bed Demand Analytics

![Bed Capacity & Occupancy Dashboard](Horizon_Health_Network/screenshots/Bed_Capacity_Occupancy.png)

### Overview

End-to-end healthcare analytics project focused on **hospital bed demand, capacity, occupancy, staffing, patient flow, and operational planning**.

The analysis covers **37 hospitals and 200,000 admissions**, providing a data-driven view of how patient demand, bed availability, staffing levels, and safe staffing compliance interact across the hospital network.

### Key Performance Indicators

| KPI | Result |
|---|---:|
| Hospitals | 37 |
| Admissions Analysed | 200,000 |
| Average Bed Occupancy | 86.2% |
| Actual-to-Planned Staffing | 86.2% |
| Safe-Ratio Compliance | 46.7% |
| Average Available Beds | 544 |
| Average Daily Admissions | 278 |
| Average Length of Stay | ~4.17 days |
| Average Closed Beds per Day | 154 |

### Bed Capacity & Occupancy

![Bed Capacity & Occupancy](Horizon_Health_Network/screenshots/Bed_Capacity_Occupancy.png)

The capacity analysis evaluates **bed availability, occupancy pressure, closed-bed capacity, and utilisation patterns** to support better understanding of hospital capacity constraints.

With average bed occupancy at **86.2%**, the analysis provides visibility into how closely available capacity is being utilised across the network.

### Admission & Speciality Demand

![Admission & Speciality Demand](Horizon_Health_Network/screenshots/Admission_Speciality_Demand.png)

The admissions analysis examines **patient demand and speciality-level activity**, helping identify patterns in hospital utilisation and areas where demand may place additional pressure on available resources.

Average daily admissions were approximately **278**, while average length of stay was approximately **4.17 days**.

### Staffing & Safe-Ratio Compliance

![Staffing & Safe Ratio Compliance](Horizon_Health_Network/screenshots/Staffing_Safe_Ratio_Compliance.png)

The staffing analysis compares workforce availability with operational requirements.

Actual staffing averaged approximately **86.2% of planned staffing**, while safe-ratio compliance was **46.7%**, highlighting an important operational area for workforce and capacity planning.

### Business Value

The analysis provides decision support for:

- Bed-capacity planning
- Hospital utilisation monitoring
- Patient-demand forecasting
- Staffing-resource planning
- Safe staffing assessment

## 🏥 NorthBridge Health Services Analytics

![NorthBridge Executive Dashboard](NorthBridge_Analytics/screenshots/executive-dashboard.png)

### Overview

End-to-end healthcare administrative service-desk analytics project analysing **ticket performance, SLA compliance, backlog, response times, resolution performance, escalation, and operational workload**.

The project analyses **3,500 service tickets across 114 agents and 72 clients** to identify service-delivery bottlenecks, backlog risk, SLA performance, and opportunities for operational improvement.

### Key Performance Indicators

| KPI | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Tickets | 3,500 |
| Resolved Tickets | 1,317 |
| Open Tickets | 2,183 |
| Resolution Rate | 37.6% |
| SLA Breached | 754 |
| SLA Breach Rate | 21.5% |
| Avg. First Response | 5.06 hrs |
| Avg. Resolution Time | 40.86 hrs |
| Escalated Tickets | 464 |
| Escalation Rate | 13.3% |
| Open Past SLA | 2,180 |
| Critical Backlog | 2,158 |
| Avg. Backlog Age | 409.2 days |

### Key Findings

- Only **37.6% of tickets** were resolved during the reporting period.
- **754 tickets (21.5%)** breached SLA.
- **2,180 of 2,183 open tickets** were already past SLA.
- The critical backlog reached **2,158 tickets**.
- Average backlog age was approximately **409.2 days**.
- Average first-response time was **5.06 hours**.
- Average resolution time was **40.86 hours**.
- **464 tickets** were escalated, representing **13.3%** of all tickets.

### Business Value

The analysis highlights opportunities to improve:

- Backlog management
- SLA compliance
- Ticket prioritisation
- Agent workload management
- Escalation processes
- Response and resolution performance
- Service-desk operational efficiency
